down
out 26 june 2026  ·  independent  ·  brisbane, au

"Down" takes a word that means defeated and turns it into willingness: the freedom of having nothing left to lose and going again anyway. A lush, upbeat indie anthem, a sandy shaker groove and alt pop-rock guitars opening into orchestral and electronic swells, all building to an explosive guitar solo. A droning synth nods to Silverchair's "Straight Lines."

Underneath the momentum, it is about keeping a childlike wonder in a world built to numb it. Saying yes to everything.

about boffie

Boffie is Sam Lawson, a Brisbane artist who makes music you can step inside. Each record is an entire world built sound by sound, written, performed, produced, mixed and mastered by him alone. "Down" is the lead single from his second album, Housesitting, and the first door into it.
